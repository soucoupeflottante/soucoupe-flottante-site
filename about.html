About

Soucoupe Flottante is a neighbourhood café designed to change with the rhythm of the day.

In the morning, it is about proper Italian-style coffee and fresh pastries made daily. At lunchtime, the food is simple, seasonal and mostly vegetarian, using products sourced from independent makers and local producers whenever possible. In the evening, the atmosphere shifts again, with craft beer, wines from small producers, food to share and a programme of live events.

The principle stays the same throughout the day: work with people who care about what they make, serve good products at fair prices, and keep the place accessible.

Soucoupe Flottante is also a cultural and neighbourhood space. Its small open stage hosts music, performances and other live events. The walls are used for rotating exhibitions, and workshops are organised for children, teenagers and adults: music, cooking, DJing, making, science, art and more.

A café, a canteen, a bar, and a place to make things, learn, listen and meet.
